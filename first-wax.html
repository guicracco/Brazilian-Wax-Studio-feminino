A personal, professional approach

Your first Brazilian wax? You are in experienced hands.

You do not need to know the terminology or arrive knowing exactly what you want. We begin with a short conversation: your chosen treatment, the areas you would like treated, your skin and any worries. You can ask me to pause or stop at any point.

Before your appointment

  • Let your hair grow to about 6 mm (a quarter of an inch). If you have recently shaved, wait a little longer.
  • Arrive with clean skin and avoid heavy oils or creams on the day.
  • Avoid harsh exfoliation just before your visit.
  • Tell me about skin sensitivities, recent skin treatments, allergies or medication that may affect your skin.

Common questions

  • Will it hurt? It can be uncomfortable, especially the first time. I cannot promise a pain-free wax, but I work carefully and listen.
  • What if I feel embarrassed? You do not need to apologise for your body, your hair or your questions.
  • Can I change my mind? Yes, at any time, for any area.
